# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS with Tailwind CSS loaded from a CDN. No build step and no bundler (decided by the user). Deployed on GitHub Pages with the custom domain `teocode.com.br` (`CNAME` at the repo root).

## Users

Owners of small and medium local businesses in Mauá (SP) and the surrounding region who do not work with technology. They arrive from Google, WhatsApp or a referral and need to decide quickly whether TeoCode is trustworthy enough to start a WhatsApp conversation.

## Product Purpose

TeoCode is a digital agency based in Mauá, SP (listed on Google as "Agência de marketing digital"). It builds personalized websites and e-commerce stores, AI agents that handle customer service and sales on WhatsApp, SEO, website maintenance and digital marketing. The site exists to turn a visit into a WhatsApp conversation, ideally a booked free evaluation ("avaliação gratuita").

## Positioning

A local agency a business owner can reach directly on WhatsApp, with a registered company (CNPJ 41.061.643/0001-22), a physical address in Mauá and post-launch support.

## Operating Context

All contact is WhatsApp: (11) 92637-7723. Hours: Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00, closed on weekends. The visitor is usually on a phone. Analytics is GoatCounter (cookieless) at `https://teocode.goatcounter.com/count`; every WhatsApp CTA records a named click event.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services (original copy, keep the meaning): Criação de Sites, Agentes de IA, Manutenção, SEO, E-commerce, Marketing Digital.
- Process: Contato inicial, Planejamento, Desenvolvimento, Lançamento.
- Facts from the FAQ: institutional sites take 7 to 15 business days; e-commerce and complex projects 3 to 6 weeks; domain and hosting can be handled by TeoCode or use the client's own; all sites are responsive; post-launch support via WhatsApp plus monthly maintenance plans (security updates, bug fixes, small content changes); an AI agent is trained with the business's information and answers customers on WhatsApp, qualifies leads and books appointments.
- Contact e-mail: contato@teocode.com.br (confirmed by the user as active).
- Offer structure decided with the user, without prices: Avaliação gratuita, Projeto de site, Manutenção mensal.
- No prices exist anywhere. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. Trust comes from verifiable facts (CNPJ, address, hours, direct WhatsApp), never from invented proof.
2. One action everywhere: start a WhatsApp conversation, with the message matching the section it was clicked from.
3. Say what each service does in plain language a non-technical owner understands.
4. Preserve the brand assets and palette exactly; evolve the execution, not the identity.
